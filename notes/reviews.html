#REVIEWS

The Pursuit of Happyness (2006)

★★★★★

Weird movie. Anticipates the “Dhar Mann” (YouTube) simple-to-the-point-of-being warped moral parable propaganda, recent Zemeckis films, etc. At the same time has a deeply moving message.

The movie totally abstracts from the threat of systemic racism. Will Smith suffers the obliviousness of his white colleagues and predatory internship laws time and time again without as much as noticing them as such, is pushed to the absolute brink by hardships of various kinds, but eventually perseveres and gets a job for which he is eminently qualified.

But he also proves to himself that his will to sincerely pursue happiness in his most strongly felt and best reasoned understanding of the term simply wouldn’t go unmet by the cosmos; or that if it didn’t, it wouldn’t be on account of his lack of faith.

Will and Jaden take up 90% of the screen time and are fantastic together in these roles.

Weapons (2025)

★★★★★

Crazy movie — Nolan’s Inception experiments channelled into horror utility. The narrative disarray sets us up to be stuck with The Witch for the last 2/3rds of the movie. Like an “ideological” (Plato) 3D pop-out effect.

Various forms of hypnosis; hypnosis, self-deception as sin; contemporary “sin theatre”.

It seems like these days there’s only about one new movie a month, but the upside is that the ones they are making and that you do see feel more significant. Others are papery disposable.

Much prefer this to slick “dark mode” A24 epic era (Eggers), “du jour” casting violence-porn hero’s journey — but maybe I was too hard on it.
