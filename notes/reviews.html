#REVIEWS ***/5 — Suits Season 1 Episode 7 “Play the Man” (Aeschylus reference?) The lead guy has hair like a bird, and the older (straight in the show) lawyer is giving queen. Super tropey, sound stagey, generic; bland. Kind of interesting to learn about legal stuff a bit tho, and it’s a nice chill de-stress. It’s not exactly Mad Men but Markle sparkles.
Weapons *****/*****: Crazy movie—Nolan’s Inception experiments channelled into horror utility. The narrative disarray sets us up to be stuck with The Witch for the last 2/3rds of the movie. Like an “ideological” (Plato) 3D pop-out effect. Various forms of hypnosis; hypnosis, self-deception as sin; contemporary “sin theatre”.

It seems like these days there’s only about one new movie a month, but the upside of that is that the ones they are making and that you do see feel more significant.

Others are papery disposable.

Much prefer this to slick “dark mode” A24 epic era (Eggers), “du jour” casting violence porn hero’s journey, but maybe I was too hard on it.
