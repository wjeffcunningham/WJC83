 After practicing constructed for three months it was a refreshing change to
get to play some sealed where one is free from sligh. Tempest sealed is not
exactly my favorite because I think there is way too much luck, but still
anything is better than boring T2 that Juniors are forced to play. So, me
and a bunch of friends went down to the PTQ NY just for some fun and maybe a
few points, some practice for the LA Super Series and some good trades. I'm
not sure if I would go to New York even if I got the invite but I didn't so
I guess it really doesn't matter. With about 60 and some pretty good sealed
experience I thought I had a decent shot a top 8.

First of all, The tourney area was wayyy better than last time where it was
in a tiny little room with a sign saying "May only hold 70 people" when we
had the Tempest Pre with 200 and 100 more trading. Ugh. Anyways, we were now
in a nice open room with lots of space. We waited a little while extra (like
always) and then were told that some of the packs didn't have uncommens so
we would have to go around table to table to make sure no one got one. When
they finally get to my table I open up my starter. Cursed Scroll, Torture
Chambre and DirtCowl Wurm are my rares and my uncommens are... what? No
uncommens?? Nooo!! Well it didn't matter as we had to switch later but still...
I ended up registering an awesome deck with Winds of Rath, Combustion, Tons
of shadow beatdown, 2 Thunders, 2 Lightning Blasts, a kindle, 2 slingers.
But I passed it to a kid who didn't really know what to do with it I think.
A shame, really. I ended up getting a fairly decent U/B deck handed to me
but only 4 swamps, 4 islands so I splash red for Kindle and Fanatic.
