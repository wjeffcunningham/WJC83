This is a T2 tournament report for one I went to yesterday at my local game
store.
PreTourney Garbage
*****************
I had been Toying with a 4CU deck for quite some time but could never quite
get the perfect mix but when I removed a few counterspells and added a few
more creatures the deck seemed to run very smoothly. And with some help from
the sideboard I found I could beat other blue decks and sligh decks quite
well. I had planned on playing my cool sligh deck o the tournament when at 3
in the morning I had a sudden urge to make a deck.
I threw it together and without playtesting a game I was off to the tourney.

* 4CU Phat *
4 Waterspout - Always always ALWAYS came in handy
2 Derelor - I am a huge fan of the Derelor and it worked very well today
2 Air Elemental - I always prefer spout to this but this guy is nothing to
complain about
4 Ophidian - With all the removal in this deck the snake rocks (even more so
than usual)
4 Man-o'-War - Helped slow down the Sligh curve and is an overall awesome
creature
4 Counterspell - Do I have to explain Everything? (:
2 Dissmiss - I like this card. Late game it is so good.
3 Impulse - See counterspell explanation
4 Incinerate - Pretty good in this deck. I might take out 1 for an Impulse
3 Terror -Even with the Derelors these work well to take down opponent's
phatties
3 Quake - Weenie Control or Late game finisher - gennerally worked well.

4 Undiscovered Paradise

3 Gemstone Mine

***Round 1***
Rory Johnston
B/R Beatdown
-------------
Wouldn't you beleive it paired up against a friend first round. I thought
that only happened in tournament reports! (:
-Game 1-
He plays a lot of creatures quickly and I stop the bleading with two quakes
and a Derelor when I am at 6. I am holding 6 Land am trying to bluff a
counterspell. Eventually on his last turn to live he torchs me for 6 and I
reveal my hand of all land cynically.
-Game 2-
After me siding in hydros and him siding in pyros nothing really changes.
I  take control early by quaking away a shadow guildmage a black knight and
a fallen askari and serve beatdown with much phat.
-Game 3-
This game was a joke. First I quake some creatures. Then I manowar three
times in a row, with an ophi out then serve bwatdown with a derelor and a
spout while incinerating his creatures.

Maybe next time Ror.
Games:2-1 Matches:1-0

***Round 2***
Jasar Elarar
Stange StOmPy
---------------
This guy is playing the strangest StOmPy I have ever seen using sentinels
barbed foliages and other oddities.
-Game 1-
He plays a first turn Rogue which after I take three beats terror. He plays
no land and promptly concedes once I start beating him down with a Elemental
and a Phid.
-Game 2-
He puts in Scragnoth  (Which I don't care about) , I put in 4 perishes. Hehehe.
I didn't even need the perished as a first turn vineyard lets me play a fist
turn Ophi, second turn spout, third turn derelor. And I terror his Scrag and
counter his Boa.

Matches: 2-0 Games:4-1
