BCHDP rules that shape the schedule

BCHDP scores project details 30, digitization and copyright 20, metadata 20, and access and preservation 30. These rules shape what has to be ready by November 27.

  • Rights (criteria, 3.02): show there are no copyright or privacy concerns for open online access, with documentation (permission letters, risk assessment, redaction or takedown plan).
  • Selection (3.01): complete before the project starts; give item counts, dates, physical description, condition.
  • Work plan (2.05) and budget (7.01): hours per activity; living wage for paid roles; project management capped at 5% of total; vendor or technician quotes attached; other funding confirmed by January 30.
  • Standards: named standards (e.g. FADGI, Dublin Core) and a recognized repository (e.g. Arca) score higher.
  • Priority this cycle goes to applications led by, or strongly partnered with, community-based non-profits.
