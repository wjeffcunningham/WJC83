BC History Digitization Program

What it's for: Digitizing BC historical material and making it freely available online. This is the closest fit for the actual scanning cost.

What changed this year: Applicants are no longer required to be a registered charity — that requirement was dropped for the 2026/27 cycle. The program is now prioritizing applicants who are community-based organizations, or who have a strong partnership with one. That's the practical reason an institutional partner (City of Vancouver Archives or similar) matters here — it's not just a nice-to-have, it's what makes the application competitive.

What the application needs to show:

  • That the applicant owns the material, or has cleared copyright to digitize it
  • A project budget with at least 50% of the requested amount matched in cash and/or in-kind contributions
  • A digitization plan and, ideally, a hosting/repository plan (the program can connect applicants to UBC's Arca repository)
  • Application form and current guidelines — both are reissued annually, so use the version posted for the cycle you're applying in

Contact: contact.bchdp@ubc.ca · Full guidelines: ikblc.ubc.ca/initiatives/bcdigitinfo/bchdp-applicant-information

Canada Council for the Arts — Explore and Create: Artistic Creation

What it's for: Not the scanning itself — this funds the artistic work built from the archive: selecting, sequencing, and presenting material as an exhibition, publication, or other creative outcome. This program absorbed what used to be two separate streams ("Research and Creation" and "Concept to Realization").

Who can apply: Jeff, as an individual Canadian citizen/permanent resident artist with at least three years of professional activity. WB and DO are not eligible to apply under this stream as individuals, since it requires a recognized professional art practice.

What the application needs to show:

  • A Canada Council client portal profile — must be created at least 30 days before applying
  • A clear artistic rationale: what the project is, why it matters as art (not just as preservation), and how it fits an existing body of work
  • Recent work samples relevant to the proposed project
  • A realistic budget and timeline

Deadline: None fixed — you can apply any time before the project start date. Decisions typically take about 5 months.

Full program page: canadacouncil.ca/funding/grants/explore-and-create

BC Arts Council

The BC Arts Council has restructured its programs since we first looked at this. There are now two separate paths that could apply here, and they're not interchangeable:

Individual Arts Grants: Media Artists

Open to Jeff directly, as an individual media artist. Grants up to $25,000. The 2026/27 intake closed June 24, 2026; the next intake date hasn't been posted yet. Worth checking back on bcartscouncil.ca/program/media-artists.

Project Assistance: Museums and Indigenous Cultural Centres

This is the stream that fits the "exhibition, public programming, collections management" side of the project — grants up to $25,000 — but it can only be applied for by an eligible organization (a museum, Indigenous cultural centre, or an arts/curatorial collective), not by an individual. This is the concrete reason the institutional partnership matters for this specific grant: without a partner organization willing to be the named applicant, this stream isn't available to us. The 2025/26 round closed October 22, 2025; the next is expected fall 2026. Lower priority for now — it belongs to the "everything else" stage, after digitization.

Program page: bcartscouncil.ca/program/museums-indigenous-cultural-centres
